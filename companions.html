My Little Companions

Three dogs who hold a special place in my heart.

Queenie

Queenie was the first dog I ever owned, out of all the dogs I have cared for. She is one of the most memorable pets I have ever had, and she will always hold a special place in my heart.

Jill

Jill is Queenie's daughter, a small and adorable Shih Tzu who is full of affection. She always knocks on my door just to sleep beside me. Her little gestures make her even more special to me.

Gucci

Gucci is Jill's daughter. She is a chubby, goofy dog who loves licking people and barking all night. Her funny personality never fails to make me laugh, and she brings so much joy to our home.
