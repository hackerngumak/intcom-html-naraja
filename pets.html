A Home Full of Paws

My family and I have always had a special love for dogs. Before the pandemic, we used to breed dogs and had around 20 dogs of different breeds, including Cane Corsos, Great Danes, Akitas, American Bullies, and Pomeranians. Growing up surrounded by dogs has given me countless memories and a love for animals that will always stay with me.
